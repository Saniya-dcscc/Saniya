Fact or fiction?

A Python-based machine learning project exploring how data can help identify potentially misleading news.

This project uses a machine learning approach to examine news content and identify items that may be misleading. The workflow includes preparing and cleaning data, training a model and refining the approach to improve its predictions.

It explores the practical stages of a data science project, from working with raw information to evaluating how a model performs.
